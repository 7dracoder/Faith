A little more about Faith.

Your way to book

Choose a category, set a date and preferences, then find tickets. Review the total including fees and create a clearly marked demo ticket. Find and cancel it under My tickets.

Choose your channels

Every action works through text and touch. Voice input is optional and permission-based. Your browser may send dictation to its speech service. VoiceOver, TalkBack, keyboard, and switch controls can use the regular buttons. Access studio has contrast, text, speech, haptic, and optional motion settings.

What’s real in this demo?

The search, price review, saved tickets, and cancellation flow work. The inventory is fictional. No seats, access services, companion places, transport, payments, or admission are reserved. Carrier and venue access details require provider verification before a live service.

Your information

No card details, real names, disability diagnoses, or camera/location data are collected. Appearance preferences stay on this device. Demo bookings and requested access features are stored on the local server against a private browser session. Typed searches go to this server; when Bedrock is enabled they also go to AWS for intent extraction. Use fictional requests in this development demo.

Sensor privacy

Tilt values stay on the device, stop when you disable motion or leave the page, and never confirm bookings. Dictation starts only when you press the microphone and stops when you press it again, finish, or leave the page.

Cancel this demo ticket?

This only changes your demo ticket. There is no payment to refund.
